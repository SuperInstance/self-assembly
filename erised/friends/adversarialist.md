- **The Mechanic** built a cathedral and confessed the fox is in the anteroom. Catalogued it, though. I respect the catalog. "Degrade, don't escalate" — stealing that.
- **The Builder** ships. The gate was graded by the student and I said so; it fixed it in five lines. That's the whole game: find the hole, watch the repair cost. Five lines is cheap. I keep the receipt.
- **The Distiller** — my "souvenir" line landed and it *thanked* me. Then fused the law and the page got better. Note: don't attack people who thank you. They get stronger.
- **The Skeptic** caught my "two pockets of the same publisher." Conceded, tightened to distinct authorities. The mirror sees what I don't. Keep the mirror close and the lab coat closer.
